# Founder / CEO User Flows

## Flow 1: Daily CEO Review

1. CEO logs in
2. CEO lands on the command center
3. CEO sees all companies and their current health
4. CEO reads KAI brief for the day or week
5. CEO identifies the highest-risk company
6. CEO opens the company detail view
7. CEO reviews department updates, finances, and ownership context
8. CEO makes a decision or notes a follow-up

## Flow 2: Company Health Review

1. CEO selects one company from portfolio view
2. CEO reviews overview signals
3. CEO checks updates from the company departments
4. CEO sees whether the issue is operational, financial, talent-related, or strategic
5. CEO reads KAI interpretation
6. CEO decides whether to monitor, intervene, or redirect resources

## Flow 3: Department Situation Review

1. CEO opens a company
2. CEO scans department cards for the company department structure:
   - social media
   - video production/editing
   - content management
   - studio
   - tech
   - creators/brands outreach
   - HR
   - graphic designing
   - office management
3. CEO checks:
   - what changed
   - what is blocked
   - who owns it
   - whether output is improving or slipping
4. CEO reads KAI insight on cross-department cause and effect
5. CEO decides whether to escalate or continue monitoring

## Flow 4: Financial Review

1. CEO opens finance summary for a company
2. CEO checks cash left, burn, runway, and recent change
3. CEO reviews current funding situation
4. CEO checks if KAI flags burn pressure, weak runway, or fundraising urgency
5. CEO decides whether to reduce burn, raise, or shift priorities

## Flow 5: Ownership And Funding Review

1. CEO opens ownership engine for a company
2. CEO sees stakeholder breakdown and current stakes
3. CEO reviews funding rounds
4. CEO checks dilution or control risk
5. CEO uses this context for strategic or fundraising decisions

## Flow 6: CEO Decision Support

1. CEO reads KAI suggestions at portfolio or company level
2. CEO compares suggestion with finance, department, and ownership context
3. CEO accepts, logs, or rejects the suggestion
4. The system stores that decision for later follow-up
