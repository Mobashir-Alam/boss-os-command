# Phase 0 Product Brief

## Product Direction

Founder OS is a multi-company operating system for a founder or CEO who oversees several businesses at once. The product starts from the CEO layer and expands downward later to support MFO, C-suite leaders, managers, and team members.

For Phase 0, the scope is limited to the CEO experience only. The goal is to define what the CEO must be able to see, understand, and decide each day without yet designing the full lower-level operating model.

## CEO Problem Statement

A CEO running multiple companies needs one place to:

- see all companies at once
- understand the health of each company quickly
- drill into what is going wrong or improving
- receive department-level updates without chasing people manually
- understand financial position, burn, and runway
- understand ownership, stakeholding, and dilution risk
- receive AI-supported strategic suggestions from a CEO perspective

The CEO should not need to open five separate tools or ask five separate departments for status just to understand the state of the business.

## CEO Vision

The CEO lands in a command center that gives:

- a portfolio view across all companies
- a health view for each company
- updates from core departments inside each company
- financial and ownership visibility
- KAI strategic interpretation and suggestions

The command center should help the CEO answer:

- Which company needs my attention today?
- What is the biggest risk across my portfolio?
- Which department is helping or hurting a company right now?
- How much cash is left and how fast are we burning it?
- Who owns what and how exposed are we in future rounds?
- What should I do next as CEO?

## CEO Scope For The First Product Slice

The CEO MVP will focus on these major areas:

- portfolio overview across different companies
- company health cards and trend indicators
- company drill-down pages
- updates from the core company departments per company
- people visibility at department level
- finance and runway visibility
- funding round and ownership visibility
- KAI founder/CEO insights and suggestions

The current departments for the CEO view are:

- social media
- video production/editing
- content management
- studio
- tech
- creators/brands outreach
- HR
- graphic designing
- office management

Each department may have around 10 members in the rough operating model, but the CEO view should begin with department-level summaries first, then allow drill-down into people when needed.

## Existing UI Signals We Are Using As Inputs

The current UI already suggests the right product direction for the CEO layer:

- Founder Command Center shows portfolio health, execution, financial position, team snapshot, growth signals, product signals, ownership signals, and KAI weekly brief
- Startup Detail shows a drill-down structure with tabs for growth, product, finances, efficiency, priorities, people, memories, documents, notes, milestones, and contacts
- Ownership and finance flows already exist conceptually in the UI and schema

Phase 0 will treat that current UI as inspiration, not final truth. We will convert the useful parts into a clean product definition.

## Success Definition For CEO MVP

The CEO MVP is successful when a CEO can:

- log in and land on a clear command center
- view all companies and their current health
- identify which company is critical, at risk, or stable
- open a company and view updates across the department structure
- understand people load and reporting at department level
- understand cash, runway, burn, and funding context
- view stakeholder ownership and funding rounds
- receive a KAI summary and actionable CEO suggestions

## Not In Scope For This Phase

The following are intentionally deferred:

- deep team-member workflows
- PM sprint management
- full functional-head dashboards
- autonomous KAI action-taking
- heavy workflow automation
- broad notification systems
- advanced collaboration UX beyond essential updates and decisions

## Product Principle

The CEO layer should be calm, high-signal, and strategic. It should summarize without hiding truth, and it should allow drill-down without forcing the CEO to live in operational detail all day.
